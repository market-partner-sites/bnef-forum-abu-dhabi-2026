BLOOMBERGNEF FORUM  |  AGENDA

Abu Dhabi

October 12, 2026  ·  Al Bateen Marina, Abu Dhabi

Energy, AI and the Next Investment Horizon

BloombergNEF's annual Abu Dhabi Forum will convene senior leaders to examine how the region's energy strategies have been shifted by the Iran war, the buildout of power infrastructure for data centers, and the evolving investment opportunities between China and the Gulf.

Joined by decision-makers from energy, capital markets and government, BNEF analysts will deliver insights on the strategies, technologies and financing models shaping the next phase of regional development.

The discussions will focus on where opportunities and risks lie, how cross-border partnerships are evolving, and the strategies that can drive business growth at the intersection of the energy transition and AI.

Panel: Building Infrastructure to Power Data Centers in the Middle East

Saudi Arabia and the UAE are emerging as premier locations for next-generation AI "superclusters," combining cheap energy, land available for development, and fast delivery. This session examines what it will take to build scalable digital infrastructure. It discusses how utilities are evolving grid and fuel strategies, what power and cooling solutions are most in need, and how developers are thinking through resiliency amidst uncertainties.

Panel: Financing the Middle East's Energy Transition

Markets in the Gulf region are pairing low cost renewables with big ambitions in hydrogen, green industrial clusters and data centers. Meanwhile, fossil fuel financing needs are rising as the region rebuilds and rewires infrastructure. This panel brings lenders and investors together to discuss the opportunities and risks in capitalizing both sides of the energy transition. How are banks and investors assessing risks of energy projects? What kind of credit support is needed? And how to establish an ecosystem to attract local as well as foreign capital for the region's transition?

BNEF Talk: Inside China's Energy Shift: New Opportunities Beyond the Barrel

China is undergoing one of the fastest and largest energy transitions in the world. This shift has taken new urgency amid heightened geopolitical volatility. Given China's evolving energy mix, how will the country shift in the next five years? What are the strategic and security considerations behind China's strategy? More importantly for Middle Eastern stakeholders navigating a disrupted energy market, how does this shift open fresh opportunities beyond hydrocarbons?

ST
Sisi Tang
Manager, China Research, BloombergNEF
3:40 PM
Panel: Middle East – China Energy Corridor: Rewiring the Future

The Middle East and China are accelerating a new phase of energy cooperation, shifting from project-level partnerships to deep industrial integration. As geopolitics reshape the energy markets and supply chains, the need to develop resilient energy systems has never been stronger. Where will the next wave of opportunities lie, across clean energy and fossil fuels? How will China's manufacturing scale and the Middle East's capital and demand strength converge? Where should investors, policymakers and developers focus next?
