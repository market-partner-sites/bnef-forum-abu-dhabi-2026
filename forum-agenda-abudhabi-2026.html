OCTOBER 12, 2026

BloombergNEF Forum Abu Dhabi

Energy, AI and the Next Investment Horizon

BloombergNEF's annual Abu Dhabi Forum will convene senior leaders to examine how the region's energy strategies have been shifted by the Iran war, the buildout of power infrastructure for data centers, and the evolving investment opportunities between China and the Gulf.

Joined by decision-makers from energy, capital markets and government, BNEF analysts will deliver insights on the strategies, technologies and financing models shaping the next phase of regional development.

The discussions will focus on where opportunities and risks lie, how cross-border partnerships are evolving, and the strategies that can drive business growth at the intersection of the energy transition and AI.
